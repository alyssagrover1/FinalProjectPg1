Welcome

Hi there! I’m Celeste, the founder and artist behind Luna Beauty. My journey into the beauty world started with a passion for creativity and a makeup brush in hand. As a self-taught makeup artist, I spent years perfecting my craft, learning through trial, error, and a lot of love for the art of transformation.

I later took my dedication a step further by graduating from cosmetology school, where I gained professional training in skin care, hair, and the science behind beauty. This combination of raw artistic passion and formal education allows me to create looks that aren't just beautiful—they're tailored to your unique features and skin type.

At Luna Beauty, I believe makeup is a form of self-expression. Whether you're prepping for a wedding, a photoshoot, or just a night out, I’m here to make you feel confident and radiant. Let’s create something beautiful together!
